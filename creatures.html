Creatures and Mobs

Every character the painter can drop - wildlife, waterfowl, insects, villagers, merchants, hostile mobs and bosses - reads one shared registry. Every mob that can be fought also reads one stat sheet.

The two registries

  • The creature registry (kind id, label, role, and its animation sheet). This is the artist's and the painter's list, so the client and the painter agree on the shape and the walk clip.
  • The mob stat sheet (level, hp, attack, strength, defense, armor, attack speed, attack range, move speed, aggro range, leash range, respawn seconds, XP reward, hostility, style and loot). This is the numbers a fight reads.

A kind in the creature registry with no row in the mob stats is scenery: it wanders, it plays its clips, and it cannot be punched. A kind with stats is a fight. Adding a mob is a row in each file, plus its sheet.

The Barrow King is the boss of the set: high HP, long respawn (45 minutes), and a six-tile magic attack range - the shape of a fight you bring friends to.

Bosses section in the painter

The map painter groups every placeable creature into families (Wildlife, Waterfowl, Insects, Villagers, Merchants, Hostile mobs, and a separate Bosses section for the Thornmother, the Wyvern and the Barrow King). A boss lives in the same registry as everything else; only the paint group changes.

Wildlife, waterfowl and insects

Not everything with legs is a fight. The registry carries the ducks, swans, ducklings, swanlings and fowl that float on water and turn to face where they swim; the butterflies, fireflies, ladybugs and grasshoppers; and the mammals - cow, deer, fox and the rest - that wander on their own clips. Every bird uses the shared water family so it floats on any water tile.

Villagers, merchants and the king

Humanoids come from the same registry: the Merchant and the Historian, King Bramblejaw the Great, and the plain goblin / skeleton / zombie / troll set. Talk and shop are authored on the placement, not on the kind. See NPCs and Shops.

The pet slot

One row in the creature registry - Rudolph the reindeer - doubles as a pet. See Pets.

Where a mob's numbers actually bite

  • Health and armor decide how long the mob takes to fall and how often your hit lands (see Combat).
  • Aggro range is how close you have to be before the mob starts moving toward you.
  • Leash range is how far it will follow before giving up and walking back.
  • Respawn secs is how long after a kill before the mob returns at full HP.

The client rolls the hit today (chance and damage from shared swing odds), so each player sees their own copy of every mob's health. The world takes over the roll in the next step of the combat runway.

Hostility

A mob reads one of three postures: aggressive (attacks unprovoked), neutral (only fights back when hit first - the wild boar is this today), or friendly (never attacks). Villagers, wildlife and waterfowl are friendly.

Saved or not

Mob positions and health are not saved. A restart returns every mob to its painted spot at full HP, and any mob that was defeated starts its respawn timer from zero.
