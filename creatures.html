Creatures and Mobs

Every character the painter can drop - wildlife, waterfowl, insects, villagers, merchants, hostile mobs and bosses - reads one shared registry. Every mob that can be fought also reads one stat sheet.

The two registries

  • The creature registry (kind id, label, role, and its animation sheet). This is the artist's and the painter's list, so the client and the painter agree on the shape and the walk clip.
  • The mob stat sheet (level, hp, attack, strength, defense, armor, attack speed, attack range, move speed, aggro range, leash range, respawn seconds, XP reward, hostility, style and loot). This is the numbers a fight reads.

A kind in the creature registry with no row in the mob stats is scenery: it wanders, it plays its clips, and it cannot be punched. A kind with stats is a fight. Adding a mob is a row in each file, plus its sheet.

What a kill pays

XP is the combat reward for the kill, the respawn is how long the mob stays down, and the drops are what lands in the bag of whoever landed the killing blow. Every mob drops bones (bury them for Holy); the cow also drops meat and a cowhide (cook the meat, see Cooking), and two Halloween mobs can drop a pet.

Bosses section in the painter

The map painter groups every placeable creature into families (Wildlife, Waterfowl, Insects, Villagers, Merchants, Hostile mobs, the Halloween groups, and a separate Bosses section for the Thornmother, the Wyvern, the Barrow King and the rest). A boss lives in the same registry as everything else; only the paint group changes.

Wildlife, waterfowl and insects

Not everything with legs is a fight. The registry carries the ducks, swans, ducklings, swanlings and fowl that float on water and turn to face where they swim; the butterflies, fireflies, ladybugs and grasshoppers; and the mammals - cow, deer, fox and the rest - that wander on their own clips. The cow is also a level-5 neutral world mob: it ambles near home, ignores you until you hit it, then fights back against you alone, and drops bones, meat (1-2) and a cowhide. The sheep, pig, goat, horse and chicken are level-1 passive farm animals that never fight back. Every bird uses the shared water family so it floats on any water tile.

Villagers, merchants and the king

Humanoids come from the same registry: the Merchant and the Historian, King Bramblejaw the Great, Emberwyn the Dark (a hooded dark wizard), and the named townies, who wander and talk but have no stat sheet and cannot be attacked. Talk and shop are authored on the placement, not on the kind. See NPCs and Shops for the roster.

The pet slot

Three rows in the creature registry - Rudolph the reindeer, the Black Cat and the Vampire Bat familiars - double as pets. See Pets.

Where a mob's numbers actually bite

  • Health and armor decide how long the mob takes to fall and how often your hit lands (see Combat).
  • Aggro range is how close you have to be before the mob starts moving toward you.
  • Leash range is how far it will follow before giving up and walking back.
  • Respawn secs is how long after a kill before the mob returns at full HP.

The client rolls the hit today (chance and damage from shared swing odds), so each player sees their own copy of every mob's health. The world takes over the roll in the next step of the combat runway.

Hostility

A mob reads one of three postures: aggressive (attacks unprovoked), neutral (only fights back when hit first - the wild boar, the cow and the stray dog are this today), or passive (never attacks; the farm animals and the bloom bunny). Villagers, wildlife and waterfowl have no stat sheet and cannot be fought.

Saved or not

Every creature with a stat sheet drops bones when defeated (one each, set per creature under Mob stats); burying them trains Holy.

Mob positions and health are not saved. A restart returns every mob to its painted spot at full HP, and any mob that was defeated starts its respawn timer from zero.
