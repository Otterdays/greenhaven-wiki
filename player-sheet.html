The Player Sheet

The sheet is one window on your character: every skill, your bag, what you are wearing, and any pet you have called in. Four keys open a tab; a fifth tab hides until the pause menu calls it.

Opening a tab

KeyTab
PSkills - level, XP into level, fill bar and total for every skill; a guide button on Woodcutting, Mining and Fishing.
IInventory - the coin bag, then every item you hold, grouped by family.
EEquipment - the twenty-slot paper doll, what is worn where, and a Take off button on every filled slot.
YPets - one card per pet you own (Summon / Send home, Take back, Give 1), with a level bar and their pack.
Pause > Premium shopShop - a hidden tab opened only from the pause menu while the store is enabled. The crystal wallet, capes, and a DEBUG top-up shelf.

Every one of the four keys is a fixed bind (like F for friends and Tab for the roster), so it cannot be rebound away. Press the key again on its own tab to close the sheet; press it on another tab to switch to it.

The Skills tab

  • Every skill has a tile: Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing, Smithing, Firemaking, Cooking, Digging, Stride, then the six combat tiles (HEALTH, Attack, Strength, Defence, Archery, Wizardry).
  • Each tile shows level, XP into the level, a fill bar, and total XP. The total level (every levelled skill added together) reads at the top.
  • Woodcutting, Mining and Fishing have a guide - a click on the tile swaps the panel body for the ladder from the same registry the game uses. Any other click, or a switch of tab, closes the guide.

The Inventory tab

The tab opens with your coin bag card at the top - tier, amount, and Drop 1 / 10 / 100 / 1,000 / all buttons. Below it, every item grouped by family: LOGS, ORES AND GEMS, FISH, BARS, TOOLS, EQUIPMENT. Wear buttons show on wearable pieces; Hold buttons show on tools that fit the Skilling slot. Sand and clay never appear as items - they have their own counters on the sheet header.

The Equipment tab

The twenty-slot paper doll (see Equipment), your armour total at the top, and one row per cape you own (see Capes). A filled slot shows a Take off button, an empty slot shows nothing, and a wearable in your bag has a Wear button that lifts it onto the doll.

Since Change Look landed, a filled slot also shows a Change look button - see Transmog.

The Pets tab

One card per pet you own: their level bar, XP number, and what they are carrying. Three buttons per card - Summon (or Send home when they are already out), Take back (return their pack to your bag), and Give 1 (offer them one stack from your bag). The Pets tab is empty on a fresh character because every pet is a gift today. See Pets.

The hidden Shop tab

The Shop tab has no button on the row of tabs. It only opens from the pause menu's Premium shop action (which is only offered while the store is enabled), and it closes on Escape like any other tab. The Shop shows every cape (both the coin one and the crystal ones), the crystal wallet, and a top-up shelf with three packs. Its header reads PREMIUM SHOP; while the store is in DEBUG, the top-up shelf header adds "DEBUG - FREE, no real money" so nobody mistakes a preview for a purchase. See Monetization.

The sheet blocks the world

The sheet is a modal: while it is open the world stops you walking, and the same key that opened it closes it. Escape closes the sheet if it is up - and closes the topmost thing if a bigger panel (the pause menu, the map, first-person) is above it, so pressing Escape one step at a time always lands you back on the world.
