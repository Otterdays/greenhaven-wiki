Accounts and Characters

Getting from the menu to standing in a world is five screens: choose a world, register or log in, pick a character, and (if you are new) make one. The path is short, and everything you save stays on the world you picked.

The world picker

The menu's first stop is a list of worlds. Each row shows the world's name, whether it is online right now, and how many players are walking it. Picking a world loads the same menu backdrop, dimmed, behind every screen that follows - so you never see a flat colour swap between the picker and the account form.

Register

  • Username and password, both case sensitive. The card says so on the form.
  • A Show password link so you can see what you typed once before committing.
  • The card fits small windows without clipping, and Enter submits the form.
  • A short countdown appears while the world writes your row to its database. The form does not time you out.
  • A successful register signs you in on the spot and takes you to character create.

Login

  • Same fields, same case-sensitive rule, same Show password link, Enter submits.
  • Remember me stores your username on this device so you do not have to retype it. The password is never stored - you still have to type it.
  • If the world does not answer within a short window, the client stops waiting and shows why, rather than sitting on a spinner forever.
  • Plain ws:// and secure wss:// both work. A secure world is checked with a real HTTPS health probe so the "online" pill only lights up when a client can actually reach the world over TLS.

Character select

Once you are signed in, the world lists every character on your account. Each slot shows:

  • The character name.
  • The total level (the same number you will see over your head in-game). See Experience.
  • A small "worn gear" preview: cape, armour, and any festive pieces you logged out wearing.

Picking a character loads its save row and drops you into the world at your last standing position. A returning character's save_version is loaded with the row, so the very first autosave after you land succeeds - a save conflict on the console is a bug, not a version issue.

Character create

A brand-new account has no character yet. Create one: a name, a face, a starting outfit. The Character Forge is the tool that made the parts; here you just pick from them.

What is saved

The world's database row for a character carries:

  • Skills. Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing, Stride, Smithing, Digging, and all six combat XP pools.
  • Wallets. Coins (see Coins) and Crystals (see Capes).
  • Items. Your bag as a JSON map of item id to count.
  • Equipment. The twenty-slot paper doll, and any worn cape and its cape.
  • Pets. Owned pets, each pet's XP, and what they carry.
  • Guild. Which guild the character is in.
  • Friends. The friend list, keyed by character id.
  • Position. Where you last stood, on which map.
  • Combat style. Accurate / Aggressive / Defensive / Controlled. See Combat.

What is not saved

Chat history, coin piles on the ground, merchant stock counts, resource node depletion, mob positions and health, first-person camera state, and the current menu screen. Those live in the running session and start fresh on next login.

Single player

Single player (Freeroam) is the same client with no world server. Your skills and worn gear persist in a local save file; coins and friend / guild state are not carried - there is no one to trade with or whisper. A fresh single-player run starts at 100 coins, the same as an online wanderer.

Version

The client's build version and the world's build version have to match to join. A mismatch is refused at the door with a plain message - the client cannot log in with an older or newer row of shared rules than the world runs.
