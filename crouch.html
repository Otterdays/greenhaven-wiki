MOVEMENT

Crouch

Hold Left Ctrl to kneel and creep at half speed. Crouch vetoes a held sprint key on both sides of the wire, so what you predict and what the world checks always agree.

What changes

  • Speed: half of walk (x0.5).
  • Sprint: vetoed while you crouch, even if Shift is still held.
  • Stride XP: none. Crouching never feeds the Warm Trail pool.
  • Breath: regenerates like a walk, not like a sprint.
  • Look: your avatar drops into a kneel pose and every other player sees it. Worn gear and capes follow the crouched silhouette.

Releasing

Let go of Left Ctrl and you stand up on the next step. Speed returns to walk (or sprint if you were holding Shift). Nothing has to be re-pressed.

Why the world owns the slowdown

Crouch is a movement contract, not a cosmetic. The world reads a "crouched" flag on your movement input, clamps your speed to the shared half-walk, and rejects any attempt to move faster than that. The kneel pose is drawn on the client, so the picture and the check are separated cleanly: the look is yours, the truth is the world's. A remote viewer sees the kneel on your avatar because it is part of your snapshot, and it never has to trust a client claim about speed.

Saved or not

Crouch is not saved. Standing or kneeling is moment-to-moment input, not a character state.
