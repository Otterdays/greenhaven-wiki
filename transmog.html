Change Look (Transmog)

Every wearable you own has a look. Change Look lets you borrow one look for a slot you are filling with something else - your platebody under a festive jumper, your bronze helm under a Santa hat - without touching the armour numbers, the recipes, or the item you actually wore.

How to try it

  • Open the Skills sheet with P and go to the Equipment tab.
  • A filled slot shows the piece you are wearing and a Change look button.
  • Press it once to cycle to the next look you own for that slot. Keep pressing and it walks back to your own look (the one that shows the piece you actually put there).
  • A good change says "You change how it looks." - the notice is the same one every viewer hears.

What can be a look

Only something you own can lend its look. That means it is either in your bag or already worn in that slot on your body. You cannot borrow a look for a piece you do not have - the world checks on every change, so a client that lies gets a plain refusal. Two slots are excluded: the Back slot (a cape's look is the cape's own field) and the Tool slot (a skilling tool is not worn, only held).

Look, not stats

A look never moves a number. Armour total, weapon accuracy and power, held recipe bonus, skill requirement - none of that follows the look. Only what you see changes, and every other player sees the same look because the world owns it. The real worn piece stays in your Equipment slot; the look is a display choice, drawn on top.

Where it is stored

A look is stored in the same Skills.equipped map that already holds your worn gear, under a key that starts with look:. Because the map is a JSON blob, no database migration is needed to add Change Look - an existing save simply had no look keys, and reads as "no look, show the piece you actually put there." A save with a look key reads as "paint this other piece over the one in the slot."

If you take the real piece off, the look goes with it: a look only shows while something is worn in its slot.

The list on the wire

Every snapshot a client receives carries the shown gear (the real worn list, with each chosen look drawn in place) - not the raw worn map. So a viewer sees a wanderer's platebody with the jumper on top, in the same place, without knowing anything about the underlying look field.

The registry fingerprint

An item's look, icon and rules come from a data file - the shared item registry - that both the client and the world load. If your copy and the world's copy have drifted (a modded or half-updated build), the world sends its registry fingerprint on join and the client says "different items" instead of pretending to name pieces it cannot see. You can still walk; you just do not see a stale tooltip on a piece that changed since your build.

How it fits

Change Look lives beside Equipment. Every look you can borrow is a piece you own; the pieces you can own come from Smithing or an admin gift (see Seasonal Content for the festive set). The overlays a look paints with are authored in the Gear Forge. Worn layers - which piece paints over which - live on Worn Gear Layers.

Saved or not

Your chosen look rides on the character save. Log out with the platebody wearing a festive jumper's look, come back tomorrow, and the jumper still paints on top.
