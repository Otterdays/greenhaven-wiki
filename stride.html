Stride

Hold Shift while moving to sprint at twice walk speed. Breath (stamina) gates the run. Stride XP comes from fresh ground under your feet - the Dust Lungs / Warm Trail rule.

How to sprint

  • Hold Shift (or gamepad LB) while moving with WASD, arrows, stick or D-pad.
  • Sprint speed is 2x walk speed (walk is 4 tiles per second).
  • Breath drains while you sprint and regenerates while you walk or stand still.
  • When breath hits empty, you drop back to a normal walk until it recovers.
  • Dust puffs spawn behind you while a sprint is active (reduced motion turns them off).

Dust Lungs / Warm Trail

Stride XP is not awarded for holding Shift in place. It is awarded when a sprint step enters a fresh map cell.

  • Enter a cell you have not warmed recently: +4 Stride XP, and that cell stays warm for 2.5 seconds.
  • Enter a warm cell again: half stamina cost, zero XP. Circling one tile does not farm levels.
  • Higher Stride level lengthens your breath pool and lowers drain a little.

The curve

Stride uses its own curve: XP to next level = 40 x current level. That is lighter than the woodcutting / mining 50 x level curve. Cap is still 99. Total XP to reach a level is 20 x level x (level - 1).

Where you see it

  • Unit frame: the second bar is labelled MP but it is breath (stamina), not mana - sprinting drains it, standing still pays it back. A Mana potion from a merchant refills +10 of it at once, and the Golden elixir does the same.
  • Hint chip: Shift / Sprint.
  • Skills sheet (P): the Stride card shows level, XP into the level, fill bar and total XP.

Saved or not

Online play saves stride_xp with your character. Breath itself is session-only and refills on join. Single player keeps Stride XP in memory only and drops it when you return to the menu.

Online: the world server owns sprint motion, breath and Stride XP. Single player runs the same shared rules locally.
