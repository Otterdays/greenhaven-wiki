Fishing

Cast at fishing spots for fishing XP. Eight tiers climb from the minnow at level 1 to the dreadfin at level 99. A catch takes a few seconds of standing still.

The ladder

Each tier is a preset on the same fishing spot tile, and one shared registry - the same values in the online game, in Single player and in the painter - owns its level gate, its XP and its cast time.

Total fishing XP sets your level on the same curve as woodcutting and mining: each step costs 50 more XP than the step before, up to the cap at 99. The Experience page has the full curve and the threshold table.

How to fish

  • You need a fishing rod and bait. Casting without a rod answers "Need a fishing rod."; without bait, "Need fish bait." Every cast spends one Fish bait. Both are Gear Forge items a merchant can stock (see Items), and the Fisherman boots are a vanity wearable that goes with the kit.
  • Click a fishing spot (tile 6) near you. Like every resource node, you must stand within 1.75 tiles, or the game answers "Walk closer to harvest."
  • Below the tier's level gate you get "Need fishing 30." (with that tier's number) and no catch.
  • A cast takes 2.5 to 5.0 seconds of standing still. Moving interrupts it: "Harvest interrupted." and nothing is caught.
  • A good cast reports "You fish the minnow. +20 fishing. +1 Minnow" and your total is saved. Catches land in the Inventory under FISHING.

Online play is checked by the world server; Single player applies the same shared cast rules locally. A spot tuned to no cast time falls back to the one-per-second instant pace shared with every other quick node ("Still working." if you click faster). The waterside card shows the spot's tier - name, level, XP and cast seconds - read straight from the same shared registry the cast rules use.

Empty spots and respawn

A fishing spot can run empty. When a map author gives a node a respawn timer, a catch that brings a fish up dims the spot to grey for every player online, and it refills by itself when the timer runs out. Clicking an empty spot answers "That node is empty for now."; finishing a cast on a spot another player just emptied answers "Someone got there first." and nothing is caught.

Depletion is shared world state: it is saved to the world database, so a server restart keeps a spot depleted until its timer expires. On the shipping map every fishing spot is infinite - minnow, no timer - so the water never runs empty out of the box.

Tiers in the painter

Fishing spots are tile 6, drawn as animated water. Authors tune a spot's tier, chance, cast time and respawn with the Resource node tool (key T): pick the fish, set level, XP and cast seconds - or change only the fields that differ and keep the tile default for the rest.

Cooking your catch

Every fish has a cooked dish that heals when eaten. Cook it at a fire, a campfire or a stove: Cooking has the ladder and the burn chances, and Firemaking shows how to light a fire with a log.

See the ladder in game

Press P during play to open the Skills sheet. The Fishing card shows your progress and a guide built from the same tier list - the same values this page mirrors.
