Combat

Six combat skills - HEALTH, Attack, Strength, Defence, Archery and Wizardry - train off their own long curve. Swing with the attack key or click a mob, and pick a style to decide what a landed hit trains.

The six skills

  • HEALTH (Hitpoints): how much damage you can take. A fresh character starts at level 10, and every hit you land feeds it a little.
  • Attack: accuracy. Higher Attack lands more of your swings.
  • Strength: damage. Higher Strength raises your max hit.
  • Defence: hardiness. Higher Defence makes you harder to hit.
  • Archery: bows and thrown weapons - accuracy and damage at range.
  • Wizardry: staves and spells - accuracy and damage with magic.

The curve

Combat skills do not use the gathering 50 x current level curve. They use the classic long curve: reaching level 2 costs 83 XP, level 10 costs 1154, and level 99 costs 13,034,431 total. Cap is 99, like every skill.

Combat level and total level

Your combat level is a separate reading: a quarter of Defence and HEALTH, plus a third of your best fighting line (Attack + Strength, or 1.5x Archery, or 1.5x Wizardry). A fresh character reads combat level 3. All six combat skills also count toward your total level, so a fresh character reads 20 overall.

How to fight

  • Press the attack key (default Q, rebindable under Settings, Controls) to swing whatever is in your weapon hand, facing the way you are turned.
  • Or left-click a living mob within reach - your move's reach plus a little slack - to swing at it.
  • Bare hands throw a punch: impact a tenth of a second in, one per second, 1 tile of reach. Clicking up to that pace lands more hits; mashing never stacks.
  • A blade slashes: a beat slower (one every 1.2 s), a little more reach (1.25 tiles), and it hits somewhat harder than a fist.
  • A landed hit does from 1 up to your max hit. Max hit comes from Strength and the weapon's power; chance to land comes from Attack against the target's Defence and armour.

Attack styles

Pick a style on the Skills tab; it is saved with your character and applies a small hidden boost while you fight that way.

  • Accurate: land more hits (+3 Attack), trains Attack.
  • Aggressive: hit harder (+3 Strength), trains Strength.
  • Defensive: harder to hit (+3 Defence), trains Defence.
  • Controlled: a bit of everything (+1 Attack, Strength and Defence), splits XP across all three.

Every landed hit also pays a third of its main share into HEALTH: 4 XP per point of damage to the style's skill, and 1 more per point to HEALTH. Bows and staves offer only Accurate and Defensive today.

Taking it back

Health climbs back on its own between fights, and it refills fully when you are respawned at the village. A Health potion from a merchant puts +10 HP straight back - use it from your bag while you are still in the scrap. A Mana potion does the same for your sprint breath, and the Golden elixir does both at once. Online, the number that moves is the world's own: the potion is spent and applied server-side, so everyone around you sees the same health bar.

What is in hand

What you fight as comes from the weapon slot on the Equipment tab: an empty hand or a plain item fights Unarmed. The candy cane sword (so far only from an admin's /give) is a Blade - the fun one: it lands hits a little easier than a fist and swings for a beat longer between hits.

Where it stands

Combat is young, and this page describes what ships today. Swings, styles, levels and XP are in; mobs take damage, show a health bar and respawn. But each player's game keeps its own copy of a mob's health - the world does not decide hits yet - and combat XP is paid in Single player only, because the online world owns skills and world-side hits are not live. Others see your swing online. Expect this section to change.

Saved or not

Online play saves your six combat XP pools and your chosen style with your character. Single player keeps them in memory only.
