Items

Everything you can pick up lives in one shared list, and one shared family rule sorts it. The game, the map painter and the panel read the same registry, so the item browser and the Inventory never disagree.

The eight families

Every item id sorts into one family. The family decides how the Inventory groups it, and whether it lists as a heading.

FamilyHeadingWhat lands here
PotionsPOTIONSAnything brewed in a bottle: an id ending in _potion or _elixir (Health potion, Mana potion, Golden elixir). This rule is checked first, before every other family.
LogsLOGSEvery log that ends in _log (young oak, pine, maple, ancient oak, yew, willow, redwood, silverwood, elderwood).
OresAndGemsORES AND GEMSEvery ore, gem, and coal the mines drop.
FishingFISHINGEverything from the fish ladder (minnow through dreadfin), plus the fishing_rod, the fish_bait a cast spends, and the fisherman_boots vanity wear - one small section for the whole trade. Cooked dishes and burnt_fish (see Cooking) sort here too.
Gathered(no heading)Sand and clay from Digging. They sit in your bag as ordinary items; the sheet keeps no separate counters for them.
BarsBARSEvery smelted bar (bronze_bar today).
ToolsTOOLSAny held tool: hammer, nutcracker hammer, bronze axe, bronze pickaxe.
EquipmentEQUIPMENTAny wearable: bronze armour, the candy cane sword, the festive set.

Every icon is one shared sheet

The item icons are one generated sprite sheet the client, the panel, the painter's Items panel and the wiki all read from. Each item has a small gear spec listing its icon rows; one build step redraws the whole sheet, so the copy in the game and the copy in the browser can never drift apart.

Where a drop or a make comes from

  • Logs come from Woodcutting - one row in the tree registry per log.
  • Ores and gems come from Mining - one row in the rock registry per ore or gem.
  • Fish come from Fishing - one row in the fish-tier registry.
  • Sand and clay come from Digging.
  • Bars and tools come from Smithing at the furnace or anvil.
  • Equipment comes from Smithing, or an admin gift.
  • Potions come from a merchant's stall. There is no brewing recipe for them yet - the doses are built into the game.

Gift-only items

A closed list marks items with no in-world source yet: the candy cane sword and the whole festive set (hat, jumper, trousers, boots). The map painter's item browser shows "Admin gift (/give)" as their source so a builder does not try to place a drop for them.

Adding a new item

One new row in the shared items registry plus one gear spec holding its icon rows; the sheet is rebuilt from those specs, never hand-painted. Family sorting is automatic (a suffix rule picks potions and logs, and a small item_family function handles the rest). If the item is worn, one new EQUIPMENT row; if it fights, one WEAPONS row; if it crafts, one RECIPES row. A potion's dose is a POTIONS row, so it restores health or breath when drunk.
