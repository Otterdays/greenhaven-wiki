Items

Everything you can pick up lives in one shared list, and one shared family rule sorts it. The game, the map painter and the panel read the same registry, so the item browser and the Inventory never disagree.

The eight families

Every item id sorts into one family. The family decides how the Inventory groups it, and whether it lists as a heading.

FamilyHeadingWhat lands here
PotionsPOTIONSAnything brewed in a bottle: an id ending in _potion or _elixir (Health potion, Mana potion, Golden elixir). This rule is checked first, before every other family.
LogsLOGSEvery log that ends in _log (young oak, pine, maple, ancient oak, yew, willow, redwood, silverwood, elderwood).
OresAndGemsORES AND GEMSEvery ore, gem, and coal the mines drop, plus anything with no family rule of its own (bones, meat, cowhide, pumpkin, candy corn, nacre and the like).
FishingFISHINGEverything from the fish ladder (minnow through dreadfin), plus the fishing_rod, the fish_bait a cast spends, and the fisherman_boots vanity wear - one small section for the whole trade. Cooked dishes and burnt_fish (see Cooking) sort here too.
Gathered(no heading)Sand, clay, flint, peat, tulip bulb, turnip, turnip seed and wheat sheaf, the farmed and dug basics from Digging. They sit in your bag as ordinary items.
BarsBARSEvery smelted bar (bronze_bar today).
ToolsTOOLSAny held tool: hammer, nutcracker hammer, bronze axe, bronze pickaxe, flint and steel, pitchfork, fishing rod.
EquipmentEQUIPMENTAny wearable: bronze armour, the festive and Halloween costumes, and every weapon.

Every icon is one shared sheet

The item icons are one generated sprite sheet the client, the panel, the painter's Items panel and the wiki all read from. Each item has a small gear spec listing its icon rows; one build step redraws the whole sheet, so the copy in the game and the copy in the browser can never drift apart.

Where a drop or a make comes from

  • Logs come from Woodcutting - one row in the tree registry per log.
  • Ores and gems come from Mining - one row in the rock registry per ore or gem.
  • Fish come from Fishing - one row in the fish-tier registry.
  • Sand, clay, pumpkins, turnips, flint, peat and bulbs come from Digging. Bones, meat and cowhide are mob drops (Creatures).
  • Bars, tools and the crossbow come from Smithing at the furnace or anvil. Costumes are stitched at the Tailor's booth, and candy corn is brewed at the witch cauldron.
  • Cooked dishes come from Cooking, and fires from a log and Firemaking.
  • The Beginner weapons are handed out by the Tutor, an NPC a builder places.
  • Potions, the fishing rod, bait and boots come from a merchant's stall that stocks them. There is no brewing recipe for potions yet - the doses are built into the game.

Gift-only items

A closed list marks items with no in-world source yet: the candy cane sword and the nutcracker hammer. The map painter's item browser shows "Admin gift (/give)" as their source so a builder does not try to place a drop for them. (The festive clothes used to be on this list; they are stitched at the Tailor's booth now.)

Adding a new item

Make it in the Gear Forge: name, family, icon, what it wears as, whether it is a weapon, a food, a log that burns or bones that bury, and its recipe. It lands in the shared item data and reaches a running game without a rebuild. Built-in items are one row in the shared items registry plus one gear spec holding the icon rows; the sheet is rebuilt from those specs, never hand-painted. Family sorting is automatic (a suffix rule picks potions and logs, and a small item_family function handles the rest). If the item is worn, one new EQUIPMENT row; if it fights, one WEAPONS row; if it crafts, one RECIPES row. A potion's dose is a POTIONS row, so it restores health or breath when drunk.
