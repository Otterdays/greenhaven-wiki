Bosses

Five bosses ship in the creature registry today (the Thornmother, the Wyvern, the Barrow King, the Pumpkin King and Vharkul). The map painter groups them under Bosses so a builder can find them fast, and the world reads the same stat rows it reads for any other mob. Nothing a boss does is a code path - it is one stat sheet with a bigger number on it.

Thornmother

A huge crowned linden matron with a heart-core of light. She is the first boss authored for Greenhaven, painted on the humanoid creature template at a bigger scale so she reads as a landmark tile.

  • Slow, plant-style walk cycle with a swaying crown.
  • Thorns, burls, lashes and a heart-core all visible from the front.
  • Aggressive - she closes when a wanderer steps into her aggro radius.

She sits on the same registry as every other hostile mob, so her stats live beside the bandit and the wyvern, and a builder can drop her anywhere. Placing her is a placement choice, not a code change.

Wyvern

A sky drake, level 18, that never lands. Every frame is airborne over a ground shadow, and its flap cycle (glide, up, half down, full down) is authored so both the idle clip and the walk clip flap. The Forge rig has no wings, so the wyvern got its own hand-painted 40 x 32 sheet.

  • Scale 1.6 - a size between a wolf and a boss.
  • Aggressive, melee style.
  • Never stands still. A wyvern that reaches its leash range walks back the same way every other mob does, but with a wingbeat.

The Barrow King

A crowned lich that floats. The ranged magic boss: the fight the game's design was first pointed at, and still the one to learn on.

He floats on a ground shadow - no feet, a hem of tatters that fades into soul-fire. Ribcage open with a light inside, iron pauldrons, gold crown, a soul-orb staff, and two soul wisps that swing a quarter turn per pose. Every one of those details is authored art on the same sheet the client draws.

A 45-minute respawn is the whole message of the stat sheet: this is a fight you bring friends to, and one you do not try again five minutes after a wipe.

How a boss is added

A new boss is one row in the creature registry, one row in the mob-stats sheet, and one hand-authored sprite sheet on the shared template. The map painter's Bosses group reads from the same registry, so a new boss appears in the picker automatically. The client and the world read the same numbers.

Where they live in the world

None of the bosses ship placed on the shipping map today. A builder who wants a boss standing in a barrow, on a hill, or in a thicket places it in the map painter and the world runs it from the same stat row everyone else does.

A fourth: the Pumpkin King

The Halloween round adds a fourth named boss, Jack Hollowgourd, the Pumpkin King (level 45, 4,200 health), the scarecrow lord of the pumpkin patch. He lives in the same creature registry; his full roster and stats are on Halloween.

A fifth: Vharkul the Glassborn

The biggest stat line in the sheet. Vharkul is an obsidian dragon: black volcanic glass with magma glowing through every crack, crouched over its lair with its wings held high. He fights in melee, so unlike the Barrow King you have to be close.
