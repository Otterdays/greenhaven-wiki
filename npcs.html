NPCs and Shops

Characters placed on a map can talk and can trade. Talking opens a branching conversation; trading opens a merchant counter that buys and sells by the unit.

Talking to an NPC

  • Walk up to a talkable character and click it. A card opens with the character's picture, name and either the greeting line or a short inspect panel.
  • A conversation is a tree of nodes. Node zero is the greeting; every option either moves you to another node or ends the chat. Every line caps at 240 characters and every option label at 48; each node offers up to 4 options, and a single conversation tops out at 24 nodes.
  • An option can also hand you a gift: any item in the game, one to 100 of them, and the author can mark it once per character so it is claimed for good.
  • Names are up to 24 characters. If the author left the name empty, the character shows the kind label (for example "Merchant").

Shops

A shop row is opened from an option marked "opens the shop" - not from a special talk. The shop counter draws one row per good with two prices and a stock line, plus a quantity box above the shelves that says how many one press moves.

FieldMeaning
Buy priceCoins the merchant charges one unit. 0 = not for sale.

InfiniteOn by default. Off means the row counts down, and the world keeps what is left.
StockStarting units when infinite is off. Ten by default; up to 9,999.

A shop offers up to forty rows. A price cannot exceed 100,000 coins. Reach to trade is 2 tiles from the merchant's saved cell - a hair wider than a harvest reach.

What a merchant can trade

Anything. A shop row names any item the game knows: logs, ore, gems, bars, fish, food, potions, tools, weapons, armour, capes, and every item made in the Gear Forge. Sand and Clay are only two of them - dug from the ground (see Digging) and taken back for coin. The painter's shop editor lists them all with a search box and category tabs - drag one onto the shelf or click it - so a stall is never fixed to a short list. Bought goods go into your Inventory like anything else.

How a trade works

  1. Walk within 2 tiles of the merchant's saved cell (a hair wider than a harvest reach), and click them.
  2. Choose the option on their dialogue that opens the shop. The client sends a shop open ask, and the world answers with the merchant's current rows and remaining stock.
  3. The counter draws one row per good: the buy price, the sell price, and how many the merchant has left (or an infinity sign for a row set to infinite). A row at 0 buy is not for sale; a row at 0 sell is not bought.
  4. Press Buy, x10, Sell or All. The client asks and the world answers with the same two things on every transaction: a Skills update (your coins and your bag both moved) and a plain Notice line - "Bought 1 Sand for 12 coins." or "Not enough coins." Buy and Sell move the number in the quantity box; x10 always lands ten; All always sells your whole stack. Click the box and type up to four digits (1 to 1,000); it resets to 1 whenever you walk up to a new merchant.
  5. A row that is not infinite counts down, and the world remembers what is left - even across a restart. It returns to its authored number when a map author changes that number in the painter. Infinite rows never change - the merchant always has another crate.

Where the coins go

Selling puts coins in your wallet (see Coins); buying takes them out, and a purchase you cannot cover is refused with "Not enough coins." What you buy goes in your bag with the rest of your things. A merchant who does not buy a good will not pay for it, whatever else they stock.

The character kinds

Anything the painter can drop is a "character kind" from the shared registry. A shop is not reserved to the Merchant kind: any placed character can carry a name, a chat tree and a shelf - the Merchant is simply the kind that looks the part. Wildlife (ducks, swans, butterflies, fireflies, ladybugs and more) can be placed but has no shop or dialogue until an author gives it one. Hostile mobs (goblin, skeleton, zombie, troll, wyvern, the Barrow King, Thornmother and the rest) can be fought and trade only if an author stocks a shelf for them.

The named townies

These characters ship in the creature registry as townies: they wander, they talk when clicked and trade if an author gives them a shelf, and nothing can attack them (they have no stat sheet). A builder places each one and gives it a name, a route and lines in the NPC panel; none is placed on a shipped map until the map's designer puts them there.

Authoring a shop

In the map painter (key N for the NPC tool): drop a character, open its inspector, and give it a name, a dialogue tree, and a shop. Options marked "opens the shop" launch the counter; unmarked options either branch to another node or close the chat. Stock, prices and infinite/counted mode are per-row, and the shelf lists every item in the game - built-in or Gear Forge - so what a merchant provides is entirely yours to set.

Saved or not

Your coins and everything you bought ride on the character save. Merchant stock is saved by the world too: a counted shelf keeps what is left over a restart, and it refills to its authored number when an author changes that number in the painter. Conversation state (which node you were on) is not saved - every close starts back at the greeting.
