Seasonal Content

The first festive round brought six new props, four festive wearables, one festive tool, the first weapon and the first pet. None of it is seasonal logic - a map simply places them, and a character simply wears them.

Festive props

Six new placeable tiles join the market prop family. Same contract as every other single-cell scenery: blocked movement, no joins, no resource node.

Tile 119 (the gingerbread house) is the current MAX_TILE - anything the painter offers lives between tile 0 and it.

Festive wearables

Four festive pieces fill the paper doll from top to toe. Every one is gift-only today: an admin hands them out with /give or the panel, and there is no in-world source yet.

  • Santa hat - Head
  • Festive jumper - Chest
  • Festive trousers - Legs
  • Elf boots - Feet

They are wear, not armour - each carries 0 armour. See Equipment.

Festive tools and the first weapon

  • Nutcracker hammer - a festive stand-in for the plain hammer at the anvil. Every recipe the hammer does, the nutcracker does too, and it pays a bonus on top. Held in the bag or in the Tool slot.
  • Candy cane sword - the first weapon in the game. A Blade with accuracy +10 and power +6, and the default Blade move "slash". Also gift-only, and its look is a held overlay on the hand.

Both pieces are marked given-only so a player who hunts them will never find a recipe or a mob that drops them.

First pet

Rudolph is the first pet in the shared pet registry: a red-nosed reindeer that fetches coins, butts your foes and carries a small pack. Also gift-only. Full rules live on the Pets page.

How a gift gets into a bag

An admin can hand any item, coin stack, cape or pet to any named character with /give <player> <item> [amount], whether the target is online on this world, on another world, or logged out. The panel's inspector has the same give form with a browseable item list. Full rules live on the Chat and Commands page.

Adding more festive content later

A new prop is one tile in the market family. A new wearable is one EQUIPMENT row plus its overlay sheet. A new gift-only item is one row in the GIVEN_ONLY list so the map-maker's item browser tells the player it comes from an admin. Nothing seasonal-code is required - a festive map is just a map with the festive tiles placed.
