Smithing

Smelt ore into bars at a furnace and smith bars into armour at an anvil. Five makes start the ladder, from a bronze bar at level 1 to a bronze platebody at level 7.

Stations

A station is just a tile on the map - no setup, no per-map configuration. Stand within 1.75 tiles (the same reach as any resource node) and click it to open the station menu, which lists every recipe that station knows.

  • Furnace (tile 113): smelts. "You smelt a Bronze bar."
  • Anvil (tile 101): smiths. "You smith a Bronze helm."

The ladder

One shared recipe list - the same rows in the online game, in Single player and in the painter - owns every make's station, level gate, XP and inputs.

MakeStationLevelXP per makeInputs
Bronze bar Furnace 1 20 1 copper ore + 1 tin ore
Hammer Furnace 1 25 1 bronze bar + 1 young oak log
Bronze helm Anvil 1 30 1 bronze bar, hammer in bag
Bronze platelegs Anvil 4 90 3 bronze bars, hammer in bag
Bronze platebody Anvil 7 150 5 bronze bars, hammer in bag

A make is all or nothing: every input leaves the bag at once, and the output and the XP arrive together. The anvil pieces need a hammer carried in the bag - it is a tool, never worn.

What the game says

  • Too far from the station: "Step closer to the furnace." (or the anvil).
  • Below the level gate: "Need smithing 4." (with that recipe's number).
  • Missing the tool: "You need a hammer."
  • Short on materials: "Need 3 bronze bar (you have 1)."

Wearing what you make

Open the Skills sheet with P and go to the Inventory: bronze pieces get a Wear button, and the Equipment tab shows the twenty paper-doll slots and your armour total. A worn piece draws on the avatar - helm, platebody and platelegs each show their own art - and Take off returns it to the bag. Swapping a slot is one click and nothing is ever lost.

The XP curve

Smithing runs on the same 50 x current level curve as Woodcutting, Mining and Fishing, cap 99, and your Smithing level counts toward your total level. The Experience page has the full curve.

Saved or not

Online play saves your smithing XP and what you wear with your character. Single player keeps both in memory only and drops them when you return to the menu.
