SKILL

Smithing

Smelt ore into bars at a furnace and smith bars into armour at an anvil. Nine makes at the furnace and anvil, from a bronze bar at level 1 to a bronze platebody at level 7, plus the tailor's booth and the witch cauldron for costumes and candy.

Stations

A station is just a tile on the map - no setup, no per-map configuration. Stand within 1.75 tiles (the same reach as any resource node) and click it to open the station menu, which lists every recipe that station knows.

  • Furnace (tile 113): smelts. "You smelt a Bronze bar."
  • Anvil (tile 101): smiths. "You smith a Bronze helm."
  • Witch cauldron (tile 165): brews candy corn from pumpkins. See Halloween.
  • Tailor's booth (tile 189): stitches every costume, all year. See Equipment for the recipes.

A make is all or nothing: every input leaves the bag at once, and the output and the XP arrive together. The anvil pieces need a hammer carried in the bag - it is a tool, never worn.

What the game says

  • Too far from the station: "Step closer to the furnace." (or the anvil).
  • Below the level gate: "Need smithing 4." (with that recipe's number).
  • Missing the tool: "You need a hammer."
  • Short on materials: "Need 3 bronze bar (you have 1)."

Flint and steel

The anvil also makes flint and steel at Smithing 1 from 5 bronze bars (hammer in the bag). It is a tool you carry to light logs into fires: see Firemaking, and Cooking for what a fire is for.

Costumes and candy

The cauldron and the tailor's booth run on the same recipe rules and pay Smithing XP, so their levels are kept low. Candy corn is level 1 at the cauldron (1 pumpkin makes 6, 4 XP). The costumes need candy corn plus pumpkins or jingle ore: Pumpkin lantern 1, Cat-ear hood 2, Santa hat 2, Witch hat 3, Elf boots 3, Festive jumper 4, Festive trousers 5, Pumpkin helm 5. Full inputs are on Equipment.

Wearing what you make

Open the Skills sheet with P and go to the Inventory: bronze pieces get a Wear button (the axe and pickaxe wear in the weapon hand), and the Equipment tab shows the twenty paper-doll slots and your armour total. A worn piece draws on the avatar - helm, platebody and platelegs each show their own art - and Take off returns it to the bag. Swapping a slot is one click and nothing is ever lost.

The XP curve

Smithing runs on the same 50 x current level curve as Woodcutting, Mining and Fishing, cap 99, and your Smithing level counts toward your total level. The Experience page has the full curve.

Saved or not

Online play saves your smithing XP and what you wear with your character. Single player keeps both in memory only and drops them when you return to the menu.
