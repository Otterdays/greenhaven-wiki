Coins

Coins live in their own bag, not in an item slot. The bag holds up to 100,000,000 coins, and the coin you see stands for how full it is: Bronze to Diamond.

The coin bag

  • A fresh character starts with 100 coins (the starter coins).
  • Hard cap: 100,000,000. A pickup that would pass the cap is refused and the pile stays on the ground.
  • The bag lives beside your items, never inside them, so it cannot be knocked out of your Inventory.

The five tiers

The amount in your bag decides the colour of the coin drawn on the sheet and the tier name shown under it. Tiers follow the bands the operator set; there is no gap after Silver.

TierAmount
Bronze1 - 9,999 (and 0: an empty bag is still Bronze)
Silver10,000 - 100,000
Gold100,001 - 1,000,000
Platinum1,000,001 - 10,000,000
Diamond10,000,001 - 100,000,000

Amount labels group thousands: "1,250" not "1250".

Where the coin bag lives

The Inventory tab of the Skills sheet (P) opens with a coin card: the tier's coin, the amount in tier colour, the tier name, and five buttons - Drop 1, 10, 100, 1,000, all. Buttons you cannot cover are greyed and the reason is in the tooltip.

Coins on the ground

A dropped pile is three coins lying flat with a small stack shadow. Everyone in the world sees the same piles. The rules below are what keeps a drop honest.

  • Reach. Click a pile within 1.5 tiles of your feet to pick it up. Too far says so; no auto-vacuum.
  • The dropper waits. If you dropped it, your own pile refuses your click for 2 seconds. Anyone else may take it at once. Without the wait, a drop meant for a friend would snap back to your own bag.
  • Whole or nothing. A pile is one amount; you either take it all or leave it.
  • Piles fade. An untaken pile disappears after 3 minutes, so the ground never silts up. The world caps its live pile list; a drop past the cap is refused instead of evicting somebody else's coins.
  • Piles are not saved. Like merchant stock, they only exist while the world runs. A restart returns every coin that was lying on the ground to whoever dropped it.

A good pickup reports "You pick up 1,000 coins." and the tier name updates on the sheet instantly if the bag crossed a band.

Coins and the store

Coins are the earn-in-world currency. The cape shop keeps one coin cape (Emerald Cape at 250 coins) so a fresh player can always clothe themselves without touching the premium path. Other capes charge Crystals, a separate wallet you cannot pick up off the ground. See Capes.

Admin gifts

An admin can hand you coins from the panel or in game with /give <you> coins <amount> (up to 10,000 per call). The gift obeys the same cap - it will not push you past 100,000,000.

Saved or not

Online: your bag is saved with your character. Single player: the bag lives in memory and resets to 100 the next time you enter a map.
