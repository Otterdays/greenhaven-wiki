SYSTEM

Experience

Total XP sets your level. Gathering and Smithing run on one curve: each level costs 50 more XP than the step before, up to the cap at 99. Stride is lighter, and the combat skills use their own long curve.

The curve

For Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing and Smithing, XP to next level = 50 x current level. Level 1 to level 2 costs 50 XP. Level 2 to level 3 costs 100. Level 10 to level 11 costs 500, and level 98 to level 99 costs 4900. Every step is 50 XP heavier than the one before.

Your level follows your total XP, not the size of the last chop. One formula gives the total XP any level needs: 25 x level x (level - 1). The table below shows what a running total buys.

Where XP comes from

Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing and Smithing share one XP curve (50 x current level to next). Stride uses a lighter curve (40 x current level). The six combat skills use their own long curve. Digging awards a separate XP pool with sand and clay counters. Chop, mine and cast awards come from shared registries - the same values in the online game, in Single player and in the painter.

Stride

Hold Shift (or LB) while moving to sprint. Fresh cells award +4 Stride XP; warm cells (2.5 s) cost half breath and grant none. Full rules live on the Stride page.

Combat

Six skills - Attack, Strength, Defence, HEALTH (Hitpoints), Archery and Wizardry - train off their own long curve, not the 50 x one. Reaching level 2 costs 83 XP, level 10 costs 1154, and level 99 costs 13,034,431 total. A fresh character starts with HEALTH at 10 and everything else at 1. Landing hits pays 4 XP per point of damage to the style's main skill and a third of that to HEALTH. Full rules live on the Combat page.

The cap

The cap is level 99 on every skill. At 99 the level bar stops climbing and total XP keeps counting with no further level. The woodcutting ladder tops out at elderwood (level 80). The mining ladder tops out at diamond rock (level 90). The fishing ladder tops out at the mooncale (level 80). Smithing today stops at the bronze platebody (level 7). Stride and the combat skills have no content ladder - only the level cap.

Levels, do not mix

  • Wanderer level is your body's legacy level. It starts at 1, and no XP feeds it.
  • Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing and Smithing are skill levels on the 50 x curve; Stride uses 40 x.
  • Combat is six separate levels (HEALTH, Attack, Strength, Defence, Archery, Wizardry) on the classic long curve.
  • Digging, coins, sand, clay, capes and worn gear are not levels - they are pools, wallets, and looks.

Where you see it

Press P during play to open the Skills sheet. Every skill has a tile - Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing, Stride, Smithing, Firemaking, Cooking, Holy and the six combat skills - showing level, XP into the level, a fill bar and total XP. Click Woodcutting, Mining or Fishing for an in-panel guide.

The bar at the bottom

The bar under the game follows your training. Gain XP in any skill - Woodcutting, Mining, Stride, Fishing, Smithing, Firemaking, Cooking or Holy - and for five seconds it shows that skill: its icon, its name, the level in the badge, XP into the level, and a bar in the skill's own colour. Go quiet and it settles on your total level and total XP, with the bar showing how far that total is toward the ceiling (every skill at 99). Each new gain brings the skill back. A level-up shines the badge and sends one glint across the bar.

Total level

Add every level together - Woodcutting + Mining + Fishing + Stride + Smithing + Firemaking + Cooking + Holy + the five combat skills other than HEALTH - and you get your total level, so a fresh character reads 13 (eight skills and five combat skills, all at 1; HEALTH trains but is not counted). Digging has no level of its own and adds nothing. Hold Tab online to see every player's total level beside their name, and the same number shows over each player's head on their name tag. You can hide the name tags over other players, or just your own, under Settings, Video, Tags.

Saved or not

Online play saves your woodcutting, mining, fishing, stride, smithing and combat XP with your character, so they survive a reload and a later login. Single player keeps XP in memory only and drops it when you return to the menu.
