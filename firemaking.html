Firemaking

Light a log with flint and steel and it becomes a fire for a while: you earn Firemaking XP, and anyone can cook on it. Eleven logs, from young oak at level 1 to the titanwood at level 95.

Getting flint and steel

Flint and steel is a tool item you carry in the bag, like a hammer. It is smithed at an anvil at Smithing level 1 from 5 bronze bars (a hammer must be carried), and it is never used up. Shops can stock it too.

Lighting a fire

Open the Inventory (P), pick a log and press Light. The fire burns on the tile you are standing on: the log leaves the bag, the XP arrives, and the fire flickers and throws sparks until its time runs out. Online, everyone nearby sees it, and anyone can cook on it. A fire never blocks walking and does not survive a server restart.

  • Open ground only: not on a doorway, archway, gate, ramp, wall, water or any prop.
  • One fire per tile, at most 3 of your own at once and 64 in a world.
  • A refusal costs nothing: the log stays in the bag.

What the game says

  • No flint and steel carried: "You need a flint and steel."
  • Below the level gate: "Need firemaking 10." (with that log's number).
  • Not a burnable item: "That will not burn."
  • Another fire on the tile: "There is already a fire here." At the caps: "You already have 3 fires burning." or "Too many fires are burning here."
  • Bad ground: "You cannot light a fire here."

The XP curve

Firemaking runs on the same 50 x current level curve as the other crafts, cap 99, and its level counts toward your total level. The Experience page has the curve. Fish go on the fire in Cooking.

Saved or not

Online play saves your Firemaking XP with your character. Single player keeps it in memory only and drops it when you return to the menu.
