SYSTEM

Settings

Settings live behind the Esc menu on five tabs. Everything here is saved per install, not per character - the world does not care what resolution you run.

Video

  • Window mode: Windowed, Borderless fullscreen, or Exclusive fullscreen. Alt+Enter toggles to fullscreen only when Alt went down within 1.5 seconds - a stale Alt from an Alt-Tab does not hijack Enter.
  • Resolution: Auto (75% of the primary monitor) or one of the fixed presets starting at 1280 x 720. A smaller choice fits a laptop; a bigger one fills an external.
  • FPS cap: Off (uncapped), 30, 60, 120, 144 or 240. Off is the default.
  • VSync: Off, Adaptive, or On. Adaptive is the default where the driver supports it.
  • Background mode: how the client behaves when the window loses focus. The safe default keeps the world running.

Audio

  • Master volume. One rail for everything.
  • Music volume. Ships at 100. See Music and Audio for what plays.
  • SFX volume. Everything that is not music: menu blips, cast tones, footfalls. Ships at 100.
  • Mute. One toggle for the whole install.
  • Mute when unfocused. Off by default. Turn it on and the world goes silent the moment you Alt-Tab out.

Controls

  • Eleven rebindable keyboard slots - movement (Up, Down, Left, Right), Player sheet, Inventory, Equipment, World map, Guild board, Crouch and Attack. A slot holds an A-Z letter or Left / Right Ctrl, Shift or Alt (Crouch starts on Left Ctrl). Player list stays fixed on Tab, F is reserved for Friends, and Esc always cancels a rebind. F5 is deliberately outside this pool so no rebind can steal the first-person toggle.
  • Cursor size. A scale on the custom in-game cursor (Normal keeps the asset size). The Twig Ladybug preview on the Gallery shows one of the shipped cursors.
  • Gamepad. The East button toggles first person; the D-pad and left stick both move; LB sprints. Rebinding the three face buttons changes which physical button East maps to.

Interface

  • Minimap corner. Top right by default; any of the four corners works.
  • Tags. Show or hide the name tag above other players, or just your own. The tag carries the character's name and total level. See Experience.
  • Reduced motion. On: dust motes, sparkles, cloud drift and the level-up sweep hold still. The world keeps walking.

Game

  • First-person look settings. Turn and pitch sensitivity, and a smooth-eye toggle. Numbers live here; the view itself is on the First Person page.

Safe reset

Reset does not clear your save or your account. It restores the shipped defaults for every setting on this page and reloads the window - a way to unstick a resolution you cannot see, or a rebind you cannot undo.

Saved or not

Settings are stored per install, not per character. Log into a different account on the same device and your settings carry over; log into your account on a different device and your settings do not.
