Prop Packs

Twenty-nine props cover a village from a market square to a Christmas scene. Every one is a single-cell, blocked-scenery tile from one shared family. A prop paints, blocks movement, and does nothing else unless it is a station.

Camp furniture

Seven more tiles build a bench run or a fire pit. The three bench-end and middle pieces share a seat band, so any 2+ run joins flush in the painter. Press R on an end piece to swap it to its mirrored twin.

  • Log bench (70) and Log bench side (71) - the flat top and side-view pieces.
  • Firewood stack (72) - blocked scenery.
  • Stump seat (73) - a single-seat tree stump.
  • Log bench end (west) (74), Log bench middle (75), Log bench end (east) (76) - the modular run.

The festive pack

Six Christmas tiles - the tree, snowman, gift pile, candy cane post, sleigh and gingerbread house - ship on the same art sheet and same family. They are documented on Seasonal Content.

What a prop is not

Props are scenery, not gameplay. They never walk, they never drop, they never harvest. Two exceptions in this whole family are the anvil and the furnace - both are Smithing stations the world reads with station_at(tile). Any crafting recipe names one of those two and any player standing within a harvest reach of a placed station can use it, no sidecar needed.

Adding a prop later

A new prop is one tile id appended, one preview function on the shared painter's market.js sheet, and one client twin. It does not need a sidecar, an author panel or a resource node. Blocked, single-cell, one art sheet - that is the whole contract.
