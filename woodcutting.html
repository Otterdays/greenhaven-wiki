SKILL

Woodcutting

Chop cuttable trees for woodcutting XP. Eleven trees climb one per 10 levels, from the young oaks on the waking trail to the ghostwood at level 90 and the titanwood giant at 95.

The ladder

Each tree is one map tile, and one shared registry - the same values in the online game, in Single player and in the painter - owns its level gate and its XP.

Total XP sets your level. Each step costs 50 more XP than the step before, so level 2 arrives at 50 total XP, level 10 at 2250, and level 80 at 158000. The Experience page has the full curve and the threshold table.

How to chop

  • You need an axe. Chopping bare-handed answers "Need an axe." The axe counts wherever you carry it - in your bag, held in the Skilling slot, or in your weapon hand - and the swing draws your own axe. Buy a bronze axe from Bartholomew Goldthread on Ottertown for 25 coins.
  • Click a cuttable tree near you. You must stand within 1.75 tiles of the trunk, or the game answers "Walk closer to harvest."
  • One chop per second on the shipping map. Clicking faster gets "Still working." and no XP - that second is shared with every other instant node, so you cannot chop, mine and dig three times a second.
  • Below the tree's level gate you get "Need woodcutting 40." (with that tree's number) and no XP.
  • A good chop reports "You chop the maple. +60 woodcutting. +1 Maple log" and your total is saved.
  • A depleted node refuses: "That node is empty for now."; walking off a channel mid-chop gets "Harvest interrupted."

Online play is checked by the world server; Single player applies the same shared chop rules locally. A chop drops one log into your Inventory (the LOGS group), and every tree on the shipping map is infinite. With the Resource node tool, an author can give any tree a chop channel, a drop chance, a batch size and a respawn timer - the same node rules as rocks and fishing spots.

Scenery trees

Two tree tiles are decoration and refuse the axe. Any node can also be switched off per cell by an author (key T, the Resource node tool), which answers "This node is turned off.":

  • English oak (tile 3): "This oak is scenery. Place a Young oak to make it cuttable."
  • White birch (tile 13): "This birch is scenery. It cannot be chopped."

See the ladder in game

Press P during play to open the Skills sheet. The Woodcutting card shows your progress and lists the tree ladder, read straight from the same registry the chop rules use.

Painter shortcuts

Placing trees in the map painter? Each tree sits on one key:

  • Young oak -, Pine J, Maple K, Ancient oak L
  • Yew G, Willow V, Redwood T, Silverwood Q, Elderwood W
  • Ghostwood and Titanwood have no hotkey yet: click their swatches in the Trees row to place them. The Palm is scenery (click its swatch) and cannot be chopped.

Burning your logs

Every cuttable log also lights into a fire: the log's level here is the Firemaking level to burn it, and better wood pays more XP and burns longer. See Firemaking.
