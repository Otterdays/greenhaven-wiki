Friends

A friend list is one account's set of characters it wants to see walking. The panel shows them live, a row opens a menu, and the chat box has two commands that add or remove someone without leaving the keyboard.

The panel

  • Live presence. A green dot means the friend is online right now; a grey dot means offline. Offline names dim so the online ones lead the eye.
  • Repaints on the tick they walk in or out. A friend joining or leaving every world that lists them shows the change immediately - the panel no longer waits for you to re-open it.
  • Words, not "here" and "away." The panel says online and offline next to each dot so screen readers and a colour-blind player both read the state.
  • Wheel scrolling. The list scrolls past the first twelve rows, and a footer tells you where you are - "1-12 of 30" - or says the list is empty.
  • Hover shading. Rows and their menu buttons both lift on hover, so it is obvious what a click will hit.

The row menu

Clicking a friend's row opens a small menu on that row, not just a whisper. The two choices are:

  • Whisper - opens the chat box with /w <name> already typed. Hidden for an offline friend - you cannot whisper someone who is not reachable.
  • Remove friend - takes the row off the list. Before the row menu existed, the only way to remove someone was to right-click their character while you were both on the same world; you can now remove an offline friend from the panel directly.

The menu stays inside the window on every screen size, so a row near the right edge does not push it off.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
/friend <name>Adds a character to your friend list. They have to be standing in this world right now - the name resolves against the local roster.
/unfriend <name>Removes a character from the list. Anyone on the list resolves - offline too.

A miss (a name that does not resolve, or one already on the list) answers with a plain line on the status strip, not a chat message. See Chat and Commands for the rest of the slash commands.

Reachable

A friend is reachable when they are online somewhere in your world list - you can whisper them even if they are on another world. An offline friend is on the panel but greyed out, and Whisper is hidden on their row.

How a friend rides the wire

Every friend row is keyed on the character's id, not their display name - two accounts can share a name, but only one owns a given id. The world answers a client's list request with the current roster; opening the panel is one cheap read, and the panel stays honest after a name change.

Adding or removing is throttled per account, so a hostile client cannot spam the database. See Guilds and Friends for the caps.
