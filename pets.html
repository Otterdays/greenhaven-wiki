Pets

A pet is a small companion that follows you, learns alongside you, and carries a few things. Today there are three: Rudolph the reindeer, and the two Halloween familiars - the Black Cat and the Vampire Bat.

Rudolph

  • Fetches coins lying near you.
  • Butts your foes - joins the mob you are fighting.
  • Carries a small pack - four distinct item stacks.
  • Trots at 4.5 tiles per second when catching up, and speeds up the further behind it falls.

Rudolph is gift-only today: no pet is a starter, and the way to get Rudolph is an admin handing it to you with /give <you> rudolph (or the panel's give form). See Chat and Commands.

Halloween familiars

Two spooky pets came with the Halloween round, and unlike Rudolph they have an in-world source: slay the matching mob for a low chance to gain it.

  • Black Cat - a witch's cat with an orange bow. It pounces on your foes and keeps a one-stack treat pouch. Drops from the Black Cat mob (8%).
  • Vampire Bat - a tiny bat that flutters at your shoulder, snatches coins and carries two things in its claws. Drops from the Vampire Bat mob (6%).

Once a familiar joins you it is yours for good, summoned from the Pets tab. An admin can still hand either one out directly.

Levels

Pets run on their own short curve, so they climb faster than your skills early. XP to next level = 30 x current level. The pet cap is level 50, well below the 99 a player can reach.

How a pet learns

  • Harvest share. Every completed chop, mine, cast or dig feeds the active pet a tenth of your XP, rounded up. Even a 1 xp dig trains.
  • Combat share. Every hit you land pays the pet 4 XP per point of damage, matching the health split on the Combat page.

The world owns the pet XP pool, so it grows the same online and in Single player.

Where a pet stands

A summoned pet appears just behind and beside its owner, and never blocks movement or collides with anybody. Its position rides on the world snapshot so every viewer sees it trotting after the same player.

Adding a pet later

A new pet is one row in the shared pet registry plus a creature-registry sheet named by that row. Ids are persisted in saves and on the wire, so a pet is appended, never renumbered.

Saved or not

Online: pet XP, held items and ownership ride on the character save. Single player keeps them in memory only.
