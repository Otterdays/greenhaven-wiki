Mining

Mine rocks for mining XP. The ladder climbs from rock and copper at level 1 to the diamond rock at level 90, with a granite boulder and a rock cluster along the way. Every rock on the shipping map never depletes - an author can give any node a respawn timer.

Total XP sets your level. Each step costs 50 more XP than the step before, so level 2 arrives at 50 total XP, level 10 at 2250, and level 90 at 200250. The Experience page has the full curve and the threshold table.

How to mine

  • You need a pickaxe. Mining bare-handed answers "Need a pickaxe." The pickaxe counts wherever you carry it - in your bag, held in the Skilling slot, or in your weapon hand - and the swing draws your own pickaxe. Buy a bronze pickaxe from Bartholomew Goldthread on Ottertown for 30 coins.
  • Click a rock near you. You must stand within 1.75 tiles of the boulder, or the game answers "Walk closer to harvest."
  • One mine per second on the shipping map. Clicking faster gets "Still working." and no XP - that second is shared with every other instant node, so you cannot mine, chop and dig three times a second.
  • Below the rock's level gate you get "Need mining 40." (with that rock's number) and no XP.
  • A good mine reports "You mine the copper rock. +30 mining. +1 Copper ore" and your total is saved.
  • A depleted node refuses: "That node is empty for now.", and if another player just took the last fish or ore: "Someone got there first."

Online play is checked by the world server; Single player applies the same shared mine rules locally. A mine drops the rock's stone, ore or gem into your Inventory (the ORES AND GEMS group), and the rock stays on the map. Map authors can turn any rock into a channel: a stand-still cast with a progress bar, a drop chance, and a respawn timer that empties the node between swings ("Harvest interrupted." if you walk off mid-cast). Sand and clay dig the same way - see Digging.

See the ladder in game

Press P during play to open the Skills sheet. The Mining card shows your progress. Click it for the in-panel guide, read straight from the same registry the mine rules use.
