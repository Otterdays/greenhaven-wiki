Cooking

Cook the fish you catch at a fire, a placed campfire or a stove. Every fish on the fishing ladder has a cooked dish that heals you, but a cook can burn it.

Stations

Stand within 1.75 tiles and click one to open the station menu, titled for what you clicked.

  • Fire: a fire you lit (Firemaking), or a campfire tile a map places. "You cook a Cooked trout."
  • Stove (tile 213): a cast-iron stove on a stone hearth, placed by a map. It cooks every dish a fire cooks and burns them half as often.

Anyone can cook on anyone's fire.

The ladder

One cook row per fish. The Cooking level to start equals the level needed to catch it, so you can cook what you can catch. Healing is HP restored when you eat the dish from the bag.

Meat

Cows drop Meat. It cooks the same way as a fish, into Cooked meat, at Cooking level 1.

Roasted bulb

A dug tulip bulb also cooks, into a Roasted bulb at Cooking level 1 (20 XP, never burns from level 12, heals 2). A raw turnip needs no fire: it heals 2 HP as it is.

Burning

At a dish's first level a cook burns 50% of the time on a fire. The chance falls in a straight line to 0 at its never-burns level. A stove keeps half of whatever the chance is, so 25% at the first level. A burn spends the raw fish, gives one Burnt fish and no XP. Burnt fish is not food.

What the game says

  • Below the level gate: "Need cooking 30." (with that dish's number).
  • No raw fish: "Need 1 trout (you have 0)."
  • A burn: "You burn the trout."
  • Too far: "Step closer to the fire." (or the stove).
  • Wrong station for a recipe: "Use a furnace for that." and the like.

Make your own dish

In the Gear Forge, New item > Cooked dish. Pick the raw ingredient, the level, the XP, how much it heals and the level it stops burning. No file editing needed.

The XP curve

Cooking runs on the same 50 x current level curve as the other crafts, cap 99, and its level counts toward your total level. See Experience.

Saved or not

Online play saves your Cooking XP and the food in your bag. Single player keeps them in memory only. Eating cooked fish heals online; Single player has no health to restore yet.
