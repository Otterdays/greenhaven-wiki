Digging

Click a patch of ground to dig it up: sand, clay, pumpkins from a vine, turnips from tilled soil, flint from a gravel pit, peat from a bog, tulip bulbs from a bulb bed. Every dig lands in your Inventory as an ordinary item, and merchants buy and sell them by the unit.

What you can dig

Seven tiles carry a diggable node, and each has one row in the shared dig registry. A dig with a chance under 100% sometimes brings up nothing; a range like +1-2 rolls each time.

How to dig

  • No tool is needed for digging today - bare hands work. (Trees ask for an axe and rocks for a pickaxe; a digging spade is not a thing yet.)
  • Click a diggable tile within 1.75 tiles of its centre - the same reach every other resource node uses. Too far gets "Walk closer to dig."
  • Digging is instant on the shipping map, so it shares the one-per-second pace with every other quick node ("Still working." if you click faster).
  • A good dig reports "You dig the sand. +5 digging. +1 Sand". Everything you dig goes into your Inventory (sand, clay and the rest sort under Gathered; pumpkins and bulbs are plain items too).

No level curve

Digging is a skill pool, not a levelled skill. Digging XP accumulates so a future ladder can hang off it, but today no level reads that pool, no gate uses it, and it does not count toward your total level. See the Experience page for the skills that do.

What the digs are for

  • Pumpkin feeds the witch cauldron and the tailor's booth (candy corn, costumes). See Halloween.
  • Turnip heals 2 HP when eaten. Tulip bulb roasts on a fire into a Roasted bulb (Cooking 1, heals 2). See Cooking.
  • Flint and peat are raw materials with no recipe yet; merchants buy them.
  • Sand and clay are what a merchant trades most simply: every stock row lists what they charge and what they pay per unit, with infinite or counted stock.

Authoring digs

Painters place the digs from their palette rows and tune a spot the same way any resource node is tuned (key T, the Resource node tool): XP per dig, chance, batch size, channel seconds and respawn. A dug clay mound with a respawn timer turns grey until it refills; sand, being walkable, stays visible but refuses the click while empty.

Saved or not

Online, the world saves your bag and your digging XP with your character. Single player keeps them in memory only.
