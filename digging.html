SKILL

Digging

Click a patch of sand or a mound of clay to dig it up. Sand and clay fill dedicated counters on your character sheet, and merchants buy and sell them by the unit.

What you can dig

Two tiles carry a diggable node, and each has one row in the shared dig registry:

MaterialTile IDWalkingXP per digDrops
Sand35walkable5+1 Sand
Clay36blocks movement8+1 Clay

How to dig

  • Click a sand or clay tile within 1.75 tiles of its centre - the same reach every other resource node uses. Too far gets "Walk closer to dig."
  • Digging is instant on the shipping map, so it shares the one-per-second pace with every other quick node ("Still working." if you click faster).
  • A good dig reports "You dig the sand. +5 digging. +1 Sand". Sand and clay do not sit in your Inventory - they have their own counters on the character sheet.

No level curve

Digging is a skill pool, not a levelled skill. Digging XP accumulates so a future ladder can hang off it, but today no level reads that pool, no gate uses it, and it does not count toward your total level. See the Experience page for the skills that do.

Merchant goods

Sand and clay are the two goods a merchant trades in today. Every stock row names one of them and lists what the merchant charges and what they pay per unit, with infinite or counted stock.

Authoring digs

Painters place sand and clay from their palette rows and tune a spot the same way any resource node is tuned (key T, the Resource node tool): XP per dig, chance, batch size, channel seconds and respawn. A dug clay mound with a respawn timer turns grey until it refills; sand, being walkable, stays visible but refuses the click while empty.

Saved or not

Online, the world saves your sand and clay counters and your digging XP with your character. Single player keeps them in memory only.
