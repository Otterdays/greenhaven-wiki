Remis Rots and Tots, Polka Dots

Open the Greenhaven main menu and pick Rots and Tots. A small, chunky, local-only buy / steal / defend challenge with the Remis cast. No server, no accounts, no payments - just you, a rival, and six silly creatures that print coins.

The loop

  1. You start with 30 Tots, the minigame's money.
  2. Walk up to a Rot standing on the market pads and press E to buy it - if you can cover its price.
  3. Carry it home. Press E again on your base pad to bank it. A banked Rot prints income every second.
  4. The rival walks a route to your base and grabs your most valuable banked Rot. Chase it down and press Space near them to bonk it loose.
  5. Lock the door with Q for ten seconds of safety, on a twenty-five second recharge. Sell a dud with X for half its market price. Buy one of five income upgrades with U at your base.

Build a six-slot collection. Bigger and sillier Rots print faster but cost more to buy and are worth more to lose.

Controls

  • WASD or arrows - move. Slower when carrying.
  • E - buy or steal the nearest eligible Rot, or bank the one you are carrying at your home pad.
  • Q at home - lock the base for ten seconds. Twenty-five second recharge.
  • U at home - buy the next income upgrade (five levels).
  • X at home - sell your lowest-income banked Rot for half its market price.
  • Space near the rival - stun them for three seconds and recover whatever they were carrying.
  • Esc - back to the menu. Your local progress in the run resets.

What this is not

  • Not online. There is no second player, no matchmaking, no shared world.
  • Not a Roblox deployment. The goal is a "dope Steal a Brainrot clone" in feel, but the game is a native Bevy minigame inside Greenhaven - it never touches Roblox's runtime.
  • No rebirth, saving, accounts or payments. A single local challenge you can pick up and put down. Closing the game keeps nothing.

Where the code lives

The minigame has its own crate under easter-eggs/remis-rots-and-tots/ with a scoped AGENTS.md for anyone working on it. It owns its rules and session state inside that crate; the Greenhaven client only mounts it from the menu. Nothing in this minigame writes to a world server, and nothing in the world writes to it.

Why it is here

Because a good, readable, chunky little buy / steal / defend loop is a fun thing to build and to play, and the Remis cast deserves a spot in the same world as the wanderers. The minigame keeps the Remis identity and original characters - it is not a re-skin of anyone else's game.
