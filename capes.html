Capes

The cape is the first cosmetic in Greenhaven. Four capes ship today: one you earn in game with coins, three premium pieces from the crystal store.

Each cape owns three colours - body, shade and trim - so it can look right in every light. Cape ids are permanent; a new cape is always appended.

Two wallets

  • Coins are earned in the world (see Coins). One cape is priced in coins so a fresh player can always clothe themselves without the store.
  • Crystals are the premium currency, bought from the crystal store. You cannot pick crystals up off the ground.

A purchase moves the coin or crystal total, adds the cape to your owned list, and never auto-equips it - you still have to say Wear.

The store is in DEBUG today

The store is reachable in game - you can walk the whole flow - but it does not cost real money yet. While the debug flag is on, every price tag on a crystal product reads "DEBUG" and both crystal packs and premium capes are free to grant. Nothing bills. A payment provider replaces the debug grant before the flag moves.

Three crystal packs exist today (Handful, Pouch, Coffer). Their prices are hidden while the flag is on; the intent is 100, 550 and 1,200 crystals respectively.

Wearing and taking off

Open the Skills sheet with P and go to the Equipment tab. Owned capes appear under the Back slot; the one you wear shows a Take off button, the rest show Wear. Only one cape sits on your shoulders at a time - a swap is one click and never costs anything.

What other players see

Your worn cape rides on every world snapshot in the same field as your name and position, so every client draws it in the same inky outline as your body. It follows your facing, walk clip, crouch, sprint, and any attack swing. A cape is a look, not a paper-doll slot - the Back slot is reserved for the cape system.

Saved or not

Both your owned list and your currently worn cape are saved with your character online, and the world validates the worn id against your owned list on load - a save that claims a cape you do not own reads as bare shoulders. Single player keeps them in memory only.
