The Wake-Up Arrival

Every arrival is a moment. Three and a half seconds of scale, rotation, glow and drifting motes tell you that you have just woken up in Greenhaven - not loaded into a menu.

What you see

  • Scale in. Your avatar starts at 60% size and eases to 100% on a cubic-out curve, so it slides into place rather than popping.
  • Lean upright. The same three and a half seconds turn you from a sideways lie to a stand on a smooth-step curve. The world reads this as one continuous arrival, not a rotation then a fade.
  • Golden aura. A two-layer glow sits at your feet the whole way up, then fades with you.
  • Motes and leaves. Twenty-four particles spiral upward from your arrival point: eighteen golden motes and six tumbling leaves. They ride a golden-angle spiral so no two tracks look alike, and they fade on their own (motes at 2.4 s, leaves at 3.2 s).
  • Text beats. Any title card or arrival copy waits a beat (15% in), fades in through the middle, and starts to fade out at the end.

How it is built

The wake-up is a client-only sequence in crates/client/src/play.rs. Three systems share the moment:

  • wake_up owns the avatar's scale and rotation, spawns the glow layers, and kicks off the particles on the first frame.
  • wake_arrive_particles animates each mote's upward drift, each leaf's tumble with gravity, and both alpha curves. It despawns on expiry.
  • avatars skips its normal walk-clip and rotation path for the waking player, so the two do not fight over the sprite.

Two settings can quiet it

  • Reduced motion in Settings skips the whole sequence. You land standing at full size. No aura, no motes, no lean.
  • Particles off keeps the lean and the aura, but does not spawn the motes and leaves.

Where you see it

Every entry into a world. Joining online lands you at the world's spawn. A single-player run lands you at your own saved position, and it still wakes you up. The one place it does not fire is a world-to-world switch on the same client - there the loading card carries the moment instead.

Timing

The sequence runs WAKE_SECS = 3.5 seconds. Every motion is on the same timeline so the glow, the scale and the rotation finish together. Motes and leaves keep drifting briefly past the end of the sequence - they expire on their own clocks (2.4 s and 3.2 s) rather than on the arrival clock.

Related

The opening menu uses the same palette of pixel mountains, drifting cloud banks, a crescent moon and star motes; that backdrop is documented on the Gallery. The in-world mood it hands off to is the world theme music on Music and Audio.
