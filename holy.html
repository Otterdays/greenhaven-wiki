SKILL

Holy

Bury the bones that fallen creatures leave behind and you earn Holy XP. Every mob in the world can drop bones, so any fight feeds the skill.

Getting bones

Bones is one item for now. Every creature with a stat sheet - from a slime to the Obsidian Dragon - drops one bone each time it is defeated, and the drop goes into the bag of the player who landed the killing hit. Loot is set per creature in the model maker under Mob stats, so a creature can drop more, fewer or none.

Burying

Open the Inventory (P), pick the bones and press Bury. One bone leaves the bag and Holy XP arrives at once. A bone is never lost to a refusal.

A new buryable item, such as bigger bones for higher levels, is made in the Gear Forge: tick Can be buried for Holy XP (bones) on the item and set its Holy level and XP.

What the game says

  • After a burial: "You bury the bones. +15 Holy XP."
  • None in the bag: "You have none to bury."
  • Below the level gate: "Need holy 3." (with that item's number).
  • Not a buryable item: "You cannot bury that."

The XP curve

Holy runs on the same 50 x current level curve as the crafts, cap 99, and its level counts toward your total level. The Experience page has the curve. Burying pops an XP chip and takes over the bottom bar like any other skill.

Saved or not

Online play saves your Holy XP with your character. Single player keeps it in memory only and drops it when you return to the menu.
