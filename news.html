The Hearth Post

Issue 2 - version 0.1.14 - 26 September to 15 October 2026 - the big one

Put the kettle back on, this one is long. Version 0.1.14 was declared on 26 September and the workshop has not stopped since: monsters fight back, the sun rises and sets, Halloween moved in wholesale, the woods came alive, a dead city and a dead village went up for the map makers, and you can now build maps together over a link. Three weeks, close to three hundred logged changes. Here is the lot, roughly in the order it matters to a player.

  • 299changes logged
  • 20+new mobs and bosses
  • 180+new tiles and props
  • 3weeks of work
Lead story

The monsters fight back

Fighting is real now. Goblins, skeletons, zombies, trolls and anything marked aggressive will spot you, walk up and hit you, and the world decides the damage, so everyone nearby sees the same fight. Your health bar counts down, refills out of combat, and at zero you wake back in the village with nothing lost.

  • Loot. Every mob drops bones (bury them to train the new Holy skill), and mobs with a loot table pay out with a "You get" line.
  • Attack style cards on the Combat tab, and a proper swing: wind-up, guard, snap, follow-through.
  • Beginner gear. A Tutor hands out a Beginner Dagger, Staff and Shortbow, one each per character; the Crossbow is smithed at level 5.
  • Tools matter. No axe, no chop; no pickaxe, no mine. The merchant sells bronze ones, plus three potions and a quantity box on the shelf.
Out in the wilds

A crowded bestiary

Skeletons, zombies and trolls were redrawn at fine detail with real attack poses, and the roster grew a lot: a Slime for first prey, Giant Beetle, Forest Sprite, Swamp Hag, Cave Spider, Stone Golem, Inferno Demon, Emberwyn the Dark, and the nature set (forest shroom, treant, giant slug, ancient wisp). The first shooters arrived too: the Thicket Archer, Mire Toad and Shard Urchin. None are placed yet. Creatures

Seasonal

Halloween moves in

A whole spooky town kit: jack-o'-lanterns, a haunted house, mausoleum, giant pumpkin, bat roosts, graveyard grounds, fog, green wisps and thunder. Seven Halloween mobs and Jack Hollowgourd, the Pumpkin King. Costumes, capes, candy corn, a bubbling witch cauldron, pumpkin vines to dig, trick-or-treat gifts, pets you can earn by slaying a Black Cat or Vampire Bat, and two lamplit townies, Boggs and Beadle Thwick the Third. A Halloween skill sits beside Christmas. Seasonal

The world

Day, night and a living wood

The sun climbs and sets and a moon rises: a full day is 24 real minutes, shared by everyone. A shader pass adds night light pools, bloom and colour grading (Settings > Graphics > Shaders). The woods got falling leaves, fireflies, butterflies, sunflecks, woodpeckers and wind, all following a painted density. Water rolls as one surface and ripples round your feet, footsteps kick up dust, and living grass blades sway over meadows.

Skills

Longer ladders

Firemaking and Cooking arrived: light logs with flint and steel, cook fish on fires and stoves. Woodcutting climbs to Ghostwood (90) and the giant Titanwood (95). Mining gained sulfur, platinum and a glowing Moonstone (99), and the gem ladder now runs ruby 20 to diamond 80. Fishing needs a rod and bait, keeps casting, and tops out at the Dreadfin (99). Firemaking - Mining

You

A new you

The character was redone with a real creator: layered looks, a feminine body, about 150 new options and MapleStory-style worn slots. The player sheet grew to 84 frames, with breathing, blinking and a true four-step sprint. Name tags are crisp pixel plates, the camera sits closer and zooms to 600%, and you walk behind high ground properly.

Social and play

Friends, guilds, billboards, a ball

Friends and Guild sit on the corner bar. Billboards show the top 10 by total level, notice boards carry news, and staff ranks come with a name badge and an in-game /admin window (F6). There is a kickable soccer ball on a pitch with goals, Rudolph fetches coins, and portals link caves and dungeons into one world. Friends

For builders

The toolbox overflowed

  • Build together: the painter shares a live room with cursors and a Share link plus join code.
  • Packs: Abandoned City, Abandoned Village (three waves), Farm and Hamlet, Spring Bloom, Victorian Town Center, harbor, Smithy and Hearth, meadow grasses, sports pitch, world-edge backdrops and portal props.
  • Props take any footprint up to 5 x 5; tile ids widened to make room. Ground quarters, the Grass Maker, cutscenes and the Gear Forge for amateurs (colour wheel, new-item wizard).
  • Harvest cards for trees, rocks and gems, and a Sort control on the palette. None of it is placed on a shipped map: placement is the map makers' job. Builder Wiki
Behind the scenes

From the workshop bench

  • Pelican is gone; the Server Admin Panel runs every world, owns the admin token, takes backups and can stop and start the database.
  • The world ticks 30 times a second instead of 20, with batched saves and faster lookups.
  • The menu fits a phone, the Android build shrank, and the game wears its own icon and name on Windows.
  • This wiki moved into the main project and got a new home page with a separate Builder Wiki.

That is 0.1.14 from the hearth. Mind the Pumpkin King on your way home.

FROM THE ARCHIVE

Issue 1 - 23 and 24 September 2026 - two busy days in the workshop

Pull up a stool. The kettle has barely stopped whistling for two days: the game moved to version 0.1.12, items stopped being code and became something you paint in a web page, the first festive season arrived early, and a lot of small rough edges got sanded down. Here is the story, in the order it is worth telling.

  • 70+changes logged
  • 12Gear Forge phases
  • 1,371tests passing
  • 6new creatures and pets
Lead story

The Gear Forge opens its doors

Until this week every sword, helm and pickaxe lived inside the program as hand-written rows. On the 24th that changed in one long push: a plan in the morning, then twelve phases through to evening. Items are now data - a list of what each item is, plus a painted look for its icon, how it sits in the hand, and how it drapes on a body - and all of it is made in the Gear Forge on the tools hub.

  • Paint, don't code. Held items are painted grids with a grip point; worn items can carry bands and little decals on the head, chest, waist, legs or feet.
  • Every body wears everything. Any humanoid, not just the player, can wear items, and held items now show during swings.
  • The item synthesiser builds a new weapon from parts - a haft, a head, a pommel - in tier inks.
  • Forge a set remakes a whole tier in another metal: names, armour, power, recipes and recoloured icons in one dialog.
  • Live reload and Try on in game. Save in the Forge and a running game client updates its pictures within a second - no restart.
  • Checks and review. Thirteen friendly checks catch mistakes before they ship, and the pinned reference pictures update themselves on save.

Alongside it came a new house rule, the GUI-first law: anything an artist or designer makes, they make from a page in the tools. Typing in the page is fine; nobody should ever have to open a file or run a command to add content.

Seasonal

Christmas comes early

Six festive props joined the painter - a Christmas tree, a snowman, a gift pile, a candy cane post, a sleigh and a gingerbread house. The five-piece festive set is complete: Santa hat, festive jumper, festive trousers, elf boots and the Candy cane sword, which is also the game's very first weapon (with its own slash).

The Nutcracker hammer does a hammer's job at the anvil and pays 10% extra smithing XP. And Rudolph, the first pet, fetches coins and carries things - he is a gift only, handed out by an admin. Seasonal - Pets

Skills

Hammers ring in the smithy

Smithing arrived with a furnace and an anvil: smelt a bronze bar, then hammer out a bronze helm, platebody and platelegs. The paper doll gained a proper equipment foundation.

The next day brought the bronze axe and bronze pickaxe - weapons that are also skilling tools, each with its own move (hack and crush) - and a Skilling slot on the paper doll, so holding a tool frees a bag space and leaves your weapon alone. Smithing - Equipment

Economy

Coins get a proper bag

Your coins now live in their own bag with five tiers - Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond - each with its own coin picture and colour. You can drop 1, 10, 100, 1,000 or the lot, and pick them back up off the ground. Coins

Out in the wilds

New faces

A wyvern (a level 18 sky drake, always airborne over its shadow) and the Barrow King, a crowned, floating lich and a level 40 boss. Smaller neighbours too: the insects were redrawn with real animation, and a ladybug, a bumblebee and a dragonfly moved in. None are placed yet - that is for the map makers. Creatures - Bosses

Social

Friends feel closer

The friends panel is live now: a green or grey dot per friend, a line in chat when someone walks in or heads home, a menu on each row, a list that scrolls past the first twelve, and /friend and /unfriend from the chat box. Friends

Mending

Fixes around the hearth

  • The chat box shows what you type again.
  • Character select lists your saved characters after login, with total level.
  • Login: show password, Enter submits, remember password, sign in straight after making an account.
  • Returning characters' saves no longer clash and get dropped.
  • No more flashes between the world picker and the account screens.
  • Plain Enter no longer flips fullscreen after an Alt+Tab.
The road ahead

Lanterns lit on the road to the worlds

Version 0.1.12 was declared on the 23rd and the live world moved to it the same day, saves intact. The player build now joins world 1 through the tunnel at play.getlullpod.com, and the world picker shows each world as online with its player count instead of a bare address. A fresh installer was built too. All of this points at the debut on 27 September, hosted from a small home PC. Worlds

Behind the scenes

From the workshop bench

  • The Character Forge opens again, and its dialog keeps your character in view while you change parts.
  • This wiki caught up: twenty rocks on Mining, new Smithing, Combat, Friends and Crash Codes pages, and a softer, homelier look with a sidebar.

That is all from the hearth for now. Mind the wyvern on your way home.
